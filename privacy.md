Privacy Policy – streamsdemise

streamsdemise is used only by its owner. When you sign in with TikTok, the app receives an access token that it uses solely to upload videos you choose to your own account. The token is stored only on the owner’s computer and is never shared, sold or sent anywhere except to TikTok. No other personal data is collected. You can remove access at any time in your TikTok settings under Security → Manage app permissions. Contact: [pmontanaiii@gmail.com]

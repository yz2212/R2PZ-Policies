# R2PZ Bot – Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 31, 2026

R2PZ ("the Bot") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected and how it is used when you interact with the R2PZ Discord bot.

### 1. Information We Collect
R2PZ may collect and process:
- Discord user IDs and server IDs.
- Discord usernames and role information.
- Support ticket messages and information submitted through tickets.
- Product key delivery records and redemption status.
- Information necessary to verify purchases or authorize product access.

### 2. How We Use Information
Information is used to:
- Assign and manage Discord roles.
- Create and manage support tickets.
- Deliver product keys to authorized customers.
- Verify customer access and prevent unauthorized key distribution.
- Maintain bot security and functionality.

### 3. Information Sharing
R2PZ does not sell personal information. Information may be shared only when necessary to operate the bot, provide requested services, comply with legal obligations, or protect against abuse.

### 4. Data Storage and Security
Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

### 5. Data Retention and Deletion
Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to operate the bot and provide its services.

Users may request deletion of eligible personal information by contacting the R2PZ support team.

### 6. Third-Party Services
R2PZ operates through Discord and may rely on third-party services for hosting, payment verification, or product key delivery.

Those services may have their own privacy policies.

### 7. Changes to This Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated when bot features or data practices change.

### 8. Contact
For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the R2PZ support team through the official Discord server.

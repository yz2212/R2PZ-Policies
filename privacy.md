# R2PZ – Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 31, 2026

R2PZ respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, how it is stored, and how you can request its deletion when using the R2PZ Discord bot.

### 1. Information We Collect

Depending on which bot features you use, R2PZ may collect and store:

**Discord Information**
- Discord user IDs.
- Discord server IDs.
- Discord usernames and display names.
- Discord role assignments.

**Product Key Information**
- Generated product keys.
- Product names.
- Assigned Discord user IDs.
- Key creation dates.
- Expiration dates and durations.
- Key status, including active, expired, or revoked.

**Ticket Information**
- Ticket channel information.
- Ticket owner Discord IDs.
- Ticket messages and conversations.
- Ticket creation and closure times.
- Ticket transcripts.

**Command and Activity Logs**
- Slash commands used.
- Administrator actions.
- Product key creation and revocation activity.
- Role assignment activity.
- Relevant timestamps and user IDs.

### 2. How We Use Your Information

R2PZ uses collected information to:
- Operate and maintain the Discord bot.
- Generate and manage product keys.
- Verify product key ownership.
- Enforce product expiration dates.
- Prevent unauthorized product key usage.
- Assign requested Discord game roles.
- Provide customer support through tickets.
- Generate ticket transcripts.
- Maintain administrator and security logs.
- Investigate abuse, fraud, or unauthorized activity.

### 3. Information Sharing

R2PZ does not sell your personal information.

Information may be accessible to authorized R2PZ administrators and support staff when necessary to operate the bot, provide support, or investigate security issues.

Discord processes information according to its own Privacy Policy and terms.

### 4. Data Storage and Security

Information may be stored in the bot's database or other systems used to operate R2PZ.

We take reasonable steps to protect stored information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no online service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

### 5. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

Examples include:
- Product key records may be retained while keys are active and when necessary for licensing records.
- Ticket transcripts may be retained for customer support and dispute resolution.
- Activity logs may be retained for security and administrative purposes.

When information is no longer needed, it may be deleted or anonymized, subject to applicable legal or legitimate recordkeeping requirements.
